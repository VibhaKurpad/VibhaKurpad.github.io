# Changelog

## 2.0.0 · October 26, 2026

A brighter, more playful redesign, and tutorials matched to every skill level.

- Seven-color rainbow palette (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and pink) used in rotation, so no single color takes over.
- Self-hosted Google Sans everywhere, with Pacifico for a few script accents (both SIL Open Font License, no external requests).
- An animated pun ticker with new wordplay, replacing the faded background captions.
- Each craft and level now has its own tutorial: 10 videos across 9 levels, each with a level-specific tip.
- Every making day in the timeline links to the exact video chapters for that day's work.
- An inline, animated hero illustration; drifting color blobs; scroll reveals; playful hover states.
- A confetti burst when you make a plan. All motion respects reduced-motion settings.
- Much less copy across the planner, tabs, and footer.

## 1.0.0 · October 26, 2026

First release of Craft Happens.

- Crochet, knitting, and hand embroidery with three skill levels and 18 projects.
- One- and two-week plans tailored to 15–60 minutes per making day, with rest days.
- Supply checklists and non-affiliate shopping links.
- Curated video lessons and clearly labeled project walkthrough searches.
- Three AI-generated progress pictures for each project, with an enlarged view.
- Browser-local saved progress and a printable plan.
- Pastel styling, subtle background captions, and responsive layouts.
- GitHub Pages deployment, CodeQL scanning, dependency audits, and Dependabot updates.

Project pictures illustrate the stages rather than exact tutorial outcomes. Tutorial popularity labels are research snapshots, not live rankings. No backend, accounts, or API keys are required.
